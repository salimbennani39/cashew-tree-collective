The place

Breakfast in the garden, platters at sunset, a band on Thursday, and a playground so everyone gets to stay a little longer.

The Cashew Tree started as a café under one big tree in Bingin. Today it is a collective: a kitchen, a shop, a rotisserie and a gym, all on the same patch of the Bukit.
